The Security Awareness Platform (SAP) is an interactive training system built for security professionals, developers, SOC analysts, and government personnel who need to stay current with evolving cyber threats.


Every challenge is sourced from authoritative organizations: SANS @RISK, OWASP Top 10, ISC² body of knowledge, CISA Known Exploited Vulnerabilities catalog, and NIST NVD. The Intel Feed pulls directly from real advisories — when SANS publishes a new @RISK newsletter, new challenges appear within 48 hours.
